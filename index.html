A curated map of providers that give away model access — recurring free tiers, zero-cost model routes, one-off trial credits and runtimes you host yourself. Compare quotas side by side, then go get your own key.

What this is not

This index never collects, proxies or shares third-party credentials. Every entry links to the provider's own signup page, and the key tester only ever talks to the provider directly from your browser.

  • Always-free = renewing allowance
  • Free models = zero-cost routes
  • Trial = one-off credits
  • Local = runs on your hardware
